# Business Outputs: HK Trade Event Analytics

## What This Platform Enables

### 1. Event Planning
The event performance section shows which event categories and venues generate the
highest attendance rates and revenue. Event planners can use this to prioritise
high-performing formats and retire low-attendance categories.

### 2. Customer Segmentation and Campaign Targeting
The customer segment pie chart classifies each registration into High Value, Mid Value,
or Standard based on the fee paid per registration. Marketing teams can use these
segments to target re-engagement campaigns at lapsed high-value customers or upsell
mid-value customers to premium event tiers.

### 3. Regional Demand Forecasting
The regional table reveals which customer regions and business sectors produce the most
revenue and highest returning customer rates. This supports decisions about where to
focus event promotion and whether to run region-specific events.

### 4. Marketing Channel Optimisation
The lead source bar chart compares which channels drive the most revenue. This connects
marketing spend to pipeline quality and revenue outcome.

## How the Pipeline Creates Trust in These Outputs

- Raw layer preserves source fidelity — every number is traceable to the original file
- Staging layer enforces types and business rules — no negative fees, no future dates
- DQ checks detect nulls, duplicates, and anomalies before data reaches reporting
- Mart layer applies consistent dimensional logic — every chart uses the same definitions
- v_dashboard_master consolidates all dimensions and metrics into one source, ensuring
  filter controls apply uniformly across the entire dashboard
